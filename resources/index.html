Data Source Guide

What students should verify

Company and valuation cases

  • Annual report or 10-K source.
  • Fiscal year and reporting currency.
  • Revenue, operating income, CFO, capex, debt, cash, shares.
  • Market price date and source.
  • Assumptions accepted from instructor versus updated by student.

SPY option quote case

  • Underlying ticker and current price.
  • Expiration date, strike, call or put, and exercise style.
  • Bid, ask, midpoint, quote date/time, and source.
  • Risk-free rate, dividend yield, historical volatility, and implied volatility if available.
  • Bid-ask spread and liquidity commentary.

Model Limitation Checklists

Responsible analyst checks

DCF and company analysis

  • Terminal value dependence.
  • WACC and terminal growth sensitivity.
  • Source date and stale market data.
  • GAAP versus adjusted measures.
  • ROIC denominator and invested capital treatment.

Options and volatility

  • American versus European exercise style.
  • Dividend assumptions and timing.
  • Bid-ask spread and liquidity.
  • Historical volatility may not predict realized volatility.
  • Implied volatility is market-implied, not guaranteed.
  • Transaction costs, taxes, and margin requirements.
