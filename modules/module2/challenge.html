Responsible-use note: Default values are instructor-provided teaching assumptions. Students should verify and update assumptions using current Microsoft filings and market data if required by the instructor.
Company Context

Why Microsoft?

Microsoft is used as the focal firm because the case allows students to evaluate a large, mature company with substantial operating income, free cash flow, reinvestment decisions, valuation sensitivity to growth and WACC, and a clear connection between ROIC and long-run value creation.
