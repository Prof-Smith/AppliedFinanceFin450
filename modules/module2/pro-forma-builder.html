Forecast operating assumptions

FCF = NOPAT + Depreciation − Capex − Change in Net Working Capital
Functional relationships vs policy choices: Revenue growth, working capital ratios, depreciation, capex, and operating asset ratios are modeled as functional relationships. Terminal growth, financing mix, and valuation interpretation remain judgment/policy assumptions.
Assumption Classification

Separate functional relationships from judgment assumptions

Functional relationships
Revenue growth, EBIT margin, depreciation/revenue, capex/revenue, NWC/revenue, NFA/revenue, and operating intangibles/revenue.
Policy or judgment assumptions
Forecast horizon, long-term growth, capital structure choices, WACC, terminal value method, and recommendation confidence.
