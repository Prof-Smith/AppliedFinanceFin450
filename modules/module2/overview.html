Module Purpose

Build the valuation engine behind Module 1

Module 1 introduced the analyst workflow. Module 2 deepens that workflow by asking students to estimate the discount rate, understand capital structure, derive free cash flow, and build a pro forma DCF valuation.

Core question: Where do the DCF assumptions come from, and how should a responsible analyst defend them?
Learning Path

Module 2 sequence

Part A: Cost of Capital
WACC overview, market value of equity, net debt, cost of debt, cost of equity, tax shield, and WACC build.
Part B: Pro Forma DCF
Operating FCF, pro forma statement modeling, terminal value, mid-year discounting, sensitivity, and valuation memo.
