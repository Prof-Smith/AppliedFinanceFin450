Analyst Purpose

From capital structure to component costs

The WACC formula only becomes useful when students can defend the cost of debt and cost of equity assumptions. This studio compares cost of debt methods and cost of equity methods before sending selected values forward into WACC.

Professional check: The cost of debt should reflect the marginal expected cost of borrowing, while the cost of equity should reflect risk borne by equity holders.
