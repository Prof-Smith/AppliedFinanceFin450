Option to Expand: the value of a pilot that creates growth rights

A standard NPV calculation may reject a small pilot because the pilot alone looks unattractive. But the pilot may create the right, not the obligation, to scale later if early evidence is favorable. That right is economically similar to a call option on future project value.

Analyst purpose: Students should explain when a negative-standalone pilot can be rational because it preserves a credible expansion opportunity.
