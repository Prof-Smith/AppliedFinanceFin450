Real Options and Strategic Flexibility

Module 6 returns to corporate investment decisions and asks what traditional NPV can miss when managers have flexibility. Students evaluate options to expand, abandon, stage, defer, or time an investment under uncertainty.

Design principle: This module should connect directly to earlier DCF work while showing students why flexibility can change the investment recommendation.
