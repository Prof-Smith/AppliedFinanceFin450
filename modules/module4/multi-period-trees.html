Multi-period binomial trees and backward induction

The multi-period binomial model extends the one-period logic by building a sequence of possible stock prices and then valuing the option backward from terminal payoffs. At each node, the option value equals the discounted risk-neutral expected value of the two next-period option values.

Analyst purpose: Students should see that option pricing is not simply a formula. It is a recursive valuation process: build terminal payoffs, move backward one step at a time, and arrive at today’s option value.
