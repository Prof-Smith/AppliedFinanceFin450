Volatility: historical versus implied

Black-Scholes/Merton depends heavily on volatility. Historical volatility summarizes past return variation, while implied volatility is the volatility input that makes the model match a quoted market option price. This lab helps students distinguish a statistical estimate from a market-implied input.

Analyst purpose: Students should explain why volatility is not just another input. It is often the central judgment variable in option pricing, valuation comparison, and risk communication.

Concept

Historical volatility versus implied volatility

Historical volatility: estimated from past returns.
Implied volatility: volatility input that solves Black-Scholes price = observed option price.
Key idea: Implied volatility is a market-implied pricing input, not a forecast guaranteed to occur.
