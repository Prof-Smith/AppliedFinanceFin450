Black-Scholes/Merton pricing

Black-Scholes/Merton provides a continuous-time benchmark for valuing European options. After building option intuition through payoffs, replication, risk-neutral probabilities, multi-period trees, and American exercise logic, students now use the closed-form model and examine why volatility is the central applied input.

Analyst purpose: Use Black-Scholes/Merton to price European calls and puts, inspect the role of volatility, and compare formula-based pricing with intuition from the binomial framework.

Formula

Black-Scholes/Merton model

d1 = [ln(S / X) + (r - q + σ² / 2)T] / [σ√T]
d2 = d1 - σ√T
Call = S e^(-qT) N(d1) - X e^(-rT) N(d2)
Put = X e^(-rT) N(-d2) - S e^(-qT) N(-d1)
