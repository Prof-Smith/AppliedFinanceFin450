Options, Binomial Pricing, and Black-Scholes

Module 4 expands the syllabus from a Black-Scholes-only topic into a complete options pricing and strategy lab. Students begin with option terminology, payoff diagrams, moneyness, put-call parity, and option strategies. They then build binomial pricing intuition through replication, state prices, risk-neutral probability, and backward induction. The module culminates with Black-Scholes/Merton, volatility estimation, implied volatility, dividend adjustments, and a professional option pricing memo.

Why this structure: Black-Scholes is more meaningful after students understand payoff geometry, no-arbitrage restrictions, replication, and risk-neutral valuation. The binomial model becomes the conceptual bridge between payoff diagrams and the continuous-time Black-Scholes benchmark.
