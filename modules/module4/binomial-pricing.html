One-period binomial pricing by replication

The one-period binomial model prices an option by building a portfolio of stock and risk-free borrowing or lending that exactly matches the option payoff in the up and down states. If two assets produce the same future payoffs, the two assets should have the same current value.

Analyst purpose: Use replication to see where option value comes from before moving to state prices, risk-neutral probabilities, and multi-period trees. This page makes Black-Scholes less mysterious by showing the no-arbitrage logic in one step.
