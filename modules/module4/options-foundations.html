Option payoff logic

This first page introduces the language and geometry of options: calls, puts, exercise price, expiration, moneyness, buyer versus writer cash flows, American versus European exercise, payoff diagrams, and no-arbitrage restrictions.

Teaching note: This page intentionally comes before binomial pricing and Black-Scholes. Students should be able to draw and interpret a payoff before they price it.
