State prices and risk-neutral probability

The one-period binomial model can be priced three equivalent ways: replication, state prices, and risk-neutral valuation. This page shows how state prices convert future state payoffs into current value and how multiplying state prices by the gross risk-free rate produces risk-neutral probabilities.

Analyst purpose: Students should understand that the actual probability of the up state is not needed for arbitrage-free option pricing. Price comes from replication and discounting under risk-neutral probabilities.
