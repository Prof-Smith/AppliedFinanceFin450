The Option Desk: SPY Option Pricing and Strategy Memo

You are preparing an option pricing memo for an investment committee evaluating a SPY option quote. Your task is to verify the quote, compare the market midpoint with model evidence, interpret implied volatility, discuss liquidity and model limitations, and defend a recommendation.

Required analytical spine: quote verification → payoff and strategy logic → binomial/no-arbitrage evidence → Black-Scholes/Merton benchmark → implied volatility → bid-ask/liquidity → model limits → recommendation and reversal condition.
Educational-use note: The default SPY values are teaching inputs, not live market data. Students should update stock price, expiration, strike, bid, ask, quote date, risk-free rate, dividend yield, and volatility using instructor-approved market sources before treating the memo as an applied quote analysis.

Assessment Rubric

Module 4 case scoring guide

Quote verification and liquidity, 15%

Documents quote date, source, bid, ask, midpoint, and spread implications.

Payoff and strategy logic, 15%

Explains contract payoff, profit, breakeven, and strategic purpose.

Binomial and no-arbitrage reasoning, 15%

Uses replication, risk-neutral pricing, backward induction, or early exercise evidence accurately.

Black-Scholes/Merton and volatility, 25%

Interprets the benchmark model and compares historical versus implied volatility.

Risk, limitations, and reversal condition, 20%

Identifies model limits, liquidity concerns, and evidence that would change the recommendation.

Professional memo quality, 10%

Communicates a concise, evidence-supported recommendation with responsible stewardship.
