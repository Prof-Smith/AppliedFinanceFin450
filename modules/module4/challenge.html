Sprint 5HModule 4 capstone

The Option Desk: Price, Hedge, and Defend the Trade

You are preparing an option pricing and strategy memo for an investment committee. The committee has asked whether an observed option quote appears reasonable and how the position should be interpreted. Your task is to connect payoff logic, no-arbitrage pricing, binomial trees, Black-Scholes/Merton, volatility evidence, and risk communication into one professional recommendation.

Required analytical spine: payoff intuition → replication / risk-neutral pricing → multi-period tree → American exercise check → Black-Scholes/Merton value → volatility interpretation → recommendation.

Assessment Rubric

Module 4 case scoring guide

Payoff and strategy logic, 15%

Explains the option position, payoff, profit, breakeven, and strategic purpose.

Binomial and no-arbitrage reasoning, 20%

Uses replication, risk-neutral pricing, backward induction, or early exercise evidence accurately.

Black-Scholes/Merton interpretation, 20%

Interprets the model price, assumptions, and relation to European option pricing.

Volatility judgment, 20%

Compares historical and implied volatility and explains whether the market quote appears reasonable.

Risk and reversal condition, 15%

Identifies the key risk, hedge issue, or evidence that would change the recommendation.

Professional memo quality, 10%

Communicates a concise, evidence-supported recommendation with model limits and stewardship.
