Sprint 8BDeal economics

Synergy Analysis: what value must the deal actually create?

Synergies are often the easiest part of a deal story to promise and the hardest part to deliver. This lab helps students separate revenue synergies, cost synergies, integration costs, timing delays, tax effects, and credibility haircuts so they can judge whether the deal premium is financially defensible.

Analyst purpose: Students should explain whether estimated synergies are large enough, timely enough, and credible enough to justify the transaction premium.
How the calculation works

From stated synergy to credible present value

The deal thesis may report large synergy numbers, but a transaction recommendation should focus on the present value of credible, after-tax synergies net of integration costs. The value of synergies depends on magnitude, timing, tax treatment, execution probability, and the one-time cost required to realize them.

Annual Gross Synergy = Revenue Synergy + Cost Synergy After-Tax Annual Synergy = Annual Gross Synergy × (1 - Tax Rate) PV of Synergy = After-Tax Annual Synergy × PV Annuity Factor Credible Synergy Value = PV of Synergy × Credibility Weight - Integration Cost - Transaction Fees Synergy Surplus / Gap = Credible Synergy Value - Required Synergy

The required synergy comes from the deal premium established on the prior page. If the synergy surplus is negative, the deal may still proceed for strategic reasons, but the memo must clearly state that the financial burden of proof has not yet been met.

Student checkpoint: Which synergy claim is most fragile, and what evidence would make the claim more credible?
