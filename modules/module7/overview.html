Module 7 Construction StartedModule purpose

Deal Analysis, Capital Structure, and Corporate Restructuring

Module 7 shifts from valuing a standalone business to evaluating strategic transactions and restructuring choices. Students assess whether a deal, recapitalization, divestiture, or restructuring plan creates value after considering standalone value, premium paid, synergies, financing risk, debt capacity, and downside scenarios.

Design principle: This module should not become generic M&A theory. It should be a professional decision lab where students decide whether a transaction is financially defensible under realistic assumptions.
