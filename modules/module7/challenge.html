Deal or Restructuring Recommendation Memo

You are preparing a transaction recommendation memo for an investment committee. The committee needs more than a yes-or-no answer. It needs a defensible recommendation based on standalone value, premium paid, synergy credibility, financing structure, EPS impact, debt capacity, and restructuring alternatives.

Required analytical spine: deal thesis → standalone value and premium → synergy support → financing structure → accretion/dilution → debt capacity → restructuring alternatives → recommendation, risk factors, and reversal condition.

Assessment Rubric

Module 7 case scoring guide

Deal thesis and premium discipline, 15%

Clearly separates standalone value from the required transaction premium.

Synergy and value creation evidence, 20%

Evaluates credible, after-tax synergy value after costs, timing, and risk adjustments.

Financing and EPS interpretation, 20%

Assesses leverage, coverage, dilution, and EPS without confusing accretion with value creation.

Debt capacity and downside analysis, 15%

Tests the balance sheet under base and downside cases.

Restructuring alternative judgment, 15%

Compares proceed, renegotiate, restructure, divest, and reject paths.

Professional memo quality, 15%

Provides a concise recommendation, risk factors, and reversal condition.
