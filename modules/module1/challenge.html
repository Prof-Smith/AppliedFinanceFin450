Scenario

Adaptive Capital Partners analyst brief

You are preparing a first-pass investment review of Microsoft for an internal portfolio committee. Your job is not to make a final investment decision. Your job is to organize evidence, identify key assumptions, and defend a preliminary recommendation.

Deliverable: A concise analyst memo with a recommendation, evidence summary, valuation interpretation, risk discussion, and decision reversal condition.
1. Model the decision

Use NPV and IRR logic to explain how expected future value depends on discount rates and cash-flow assumptions.

Quantitative reasoning
2. Read the company

Use statements and ratios to identify business quality, profitability, cash generation, and balance-sheet context.

Financial diagnosis
3. Interpret valuation

Use enterprise value, net debt, EV/Sales, and EV/EBITDA to frame what the market is pricing.
