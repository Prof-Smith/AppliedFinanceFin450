Case 1 · From Cash Flows to Corporate Value

Build and Defend a Corporate Valuation

Use the Microsoft valuation workbook as a reusable architecture to analyze and value an approved publicly traded company.

Replace Microsoft’s financial data, market observations, forecast assumptions, and peer group with company-specific evidence while preserving the workbook’s formulas, links, formatting conventions, and audit controls. The goal is not simply to generate a target price. The goal is to create a transparent valuation that another analyst can trace, reproduce, challenge, and understand.

Open DownloadsReturn to Module 1

Your analysis

Collect and document historical financial statements, reporting periods, units, sources, and the valuation date. Complete the economic balance-sheet classification, market enterprise-value bridge, five-year DCF, sensitivity analysis, and company-specific comparable-company valuation.

Your judgment

Clearly distinguish reported facts, dated market observations, and analyst assumptions. Explain material balance-sheet classifications, rebuild the peer group for the selected company, and defend any premium or discount using growth, margins, cash conversion, capital intensity, competitive position, and risk.

Required deliverables

  1. Completed Excel valuation workbook
  2. Documented source register
  3. Accounting EV and Market EV analyses
  4. DCF and sensitivity analysis
  5. Comparable-company valuation
  6. Investment Review Memo

Final recommendation

Reconcile accounting invested capital, observed market value, DCF, and comparable-company evidence. Resolve material issues in the Checks tab and state the recommendation, confidence level, valuation range, principal evidence, most important uncertainty, model limitation, and measurable reversal condition.

Analyst standard: Carry forward one claim, one number, one uncertainty, and one measurable reversal condition. Preserve the architecture. Replace the evidence. Make every judgment traceable.
