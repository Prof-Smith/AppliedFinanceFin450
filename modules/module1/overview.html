What makes a financial model useful?

A useful model organizes uncertainty, clarifies assumptions, and supports a decision. In Module 1, students build the mental structure that supports every later module.

1

Learn

Develop foundational concepts from financial modeling, time value of money, valuation, and company analysis.

2

Build

Prepare for calculators, dashboards, DCF models, company diagnostics, and case work.

3

Decide

Translate results into an analyst recommendation supported by evidence, assumptions, and a reversal condition.
