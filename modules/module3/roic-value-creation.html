ROIC as the bridge between operations and valuation

The Merck Chapter 5 example uses pro forma statements to compute free cash flow and implied ROIC. This page adapts that logic to AbbVie by separating operating margin, invested capital turnover, ROIC, WACC spread, reinvestment rate, and sustainable growth.

Core idea: A company creates economic value when ROIC exceeds its cost of capital and growth can be funded without destroying future free cash flow.
Responsible-use note: Default values are teaching placeholders. Students should update NOPAT, invested capital, WACC, and reinvestment inputs using AbbVie filings or instructor-provided data before graded submission.
