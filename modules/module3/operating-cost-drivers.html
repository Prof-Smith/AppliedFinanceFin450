Operating cost projection logic from the Merck example

The Merck Chapter 5 example models operating costs as a relationship with sales, separating depreciation where necessary and using historical operating relationships to support pro forma assumptions. This page adapts that logic to AbbVie by focusing on COGS, R&D, SG&A, other operating costs, and depreciation/amortization.

Responsible-use note: Default values are teaching placeholders. Students should update costs using AbbVie filings, annual reports, or instructor-provided data before graded submission.
Inputs

Historical operating costs

Enter four years of revenue and operating cost categories. Use this evidence to justify forecast margins.

Cost Behavior Assumptions

Forecast driver model

Driver equation:
Operating cost before D&A = Fixed operating cost + Variable cost ratio × revenue
Analyst judgment: A cost ratio model is simpler. A fixed-variable model forces students to explain which costs scale with sales and which costs reflect platform investment.
