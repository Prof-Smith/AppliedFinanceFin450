Sprint 4I Active PageScenario

Investment committee company-analysis memo

You are preparing a short company-analysis memo on AbbVie. The committee does not want only a target price. The committee wants to understand the business drivers, operating economics, asset intensity, value creation, valuation evidence, and the assumption that would most likely reverse the conclusion.

Text connection: The Merck Chapter 5 example builds from historical statements to pro forma assumptions, FCF, ROIC, DCF, and multiples. This challenge asks students to apply that full logic to AbbVie.
Responsible-use note: Default output values are teaching placeholders. Students should update data using AbbVie filings, market data, and instructor-provided assumptions before graded submission.
