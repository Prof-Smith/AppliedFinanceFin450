From valuation engine to company diagnosis

Module 3 applies the Merck Chapter 5 pro forma modeling architecture to AbbVie. The module emphasizes company diagnosis: what drives revenue, how operating costs behave, how assets and reinvestment shape free cash flow, whether ROIC exceeds WACC, and whether valuation evidence supports the thesis.

Production improvement: This integrated patch restores all full Module 3 pages, uses one shared navigation system, adds data integrity panels, and preserves the final memo artifact workflow.
