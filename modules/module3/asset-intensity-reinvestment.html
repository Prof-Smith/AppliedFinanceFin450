Asset intensity logic from the Merck example

The Merck Chapter 5 example analyzes fixed assets, depreciation, and operating relationships before translating historical financial statements into forecast assumptions. This page adapts that logic to AbbVie by giving special attention to operating intangibles as well as PPE and capex.

Responsible-use note: Default values are teaching placeholders. Students should update asset, depreciation, amortization, and capex values using AbbVie filings or instructor-provided data before graded submission.
Inputs

Historical operating asset and reinvestment data

Enter four years of revenue, operating assets, D&A, capex, and NOPAT to convert the company story into reinvestment evidence.

Forecast Assumptions

Reinvestment driver model

Reinvestment relationship:
FCF = NOPAT + D&A − Capex − Change in Operating Invested Capital
Analyst judgment: Biopharma analysis should not treat capital intensity as only PPE. Operating intangibles and acquired product rights can dominate invested capital.
