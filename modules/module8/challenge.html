Market Reaction and Final Analyst Briefing

You are preparing the final analyst briefing for the course. The briefing should combine the valuation or strategic recommendation with event-study evidence. The investment committee needs a clear recommendation, a concise evidence hierarchy, an interpretation of the market reaction, and a reversal condition.

Required analytical spine: recommendation → valuation or strategic thesis → benchmark model → abnormal return evidence → event-window interpretation → market reaction dashboard → key risks → final action and reversal condition.

Assessment Rubric

Final course briefing scoring guide

Recommendation clarity, 15%

States a clear action and audience-appropriate conclusion.

Evidence hierarchy, 20%

Prioritizes valuation, strategic, risk, and market reaction evidence effectively.

Event-study accuracy, 20%

Correctly interprets benchmark model, abnormal returns, CAR, and event window.

Market reaction interpretation, 15%

Explains whether the evidence supports, contradicts, or qualifies the recommendation.

Risk and reversal condition, 15%

Identifies uncertainty and names evidence that would change the recommendation.

Professional briefing quality, 15%

Uses concise, balanced, committee-ready language.
