Module 8 Construction StartedModule purpose

Event Study, Market Reaction, and Final Analyst Briefing

Module 8 closes the course by asking whether the market reaction supports or challenges the analyst’s recommendation. Students use event-study logic to estimate abnormal returns, cumulative abnormal returns, and evidence-based interpretation around a corporate event.

Design principle: This module should not become a statistics lecture. It should help students translate market reaction evidence into a clear final analyst briefing.
