Sprint 9EMarket reaction synthesis

Market Reaction Dashboard: combine benchmark, abnormal return, and event-window evidence

This dashboard consolidates the event-study evidence from Module 8. Students compare benchmark sensitivity, event-day abnormal return, event-window CAR, materiality, and reaction pattern to decide whether the market reaction supports the analyst story.

Analyst purpose: Students should synthesize multiple market reaction diagnostics into one evidence-backed interpretation instead of relying on a single CAR number.
How the dashboard works

Evidence strength depends on direction, size, robustness, and timing

A strong market reaction interpretation is based on more than whether CAR is positive or negative. The analyst should ask whether the reaction is economically meaningful, robust to the benchmark model, concentrated around the event date, and consistent with the valuation or transaction story.

Evidence Score = Direction Score + Materiality Score + Benchmark Robustness Score + Timing Quality Score Direction Score = positive, negative, or mixed reaction Materiality Score = CAR compared with threshold Benchmark Robustness Score = abnormal return remains meaningful across benchmark models Timing Quality Score = event-day focused reaction is cleaner than leakage or delayed reaction

The dashboard converts those ideas into an interpretive signal. The signal is not a substitute for judgment. It gives students a structured way to explain how strong or fragile the market reaction evidence appears.

Student checkpoint: Does the evidence support the analyst story, contradict it, or require qualification?
