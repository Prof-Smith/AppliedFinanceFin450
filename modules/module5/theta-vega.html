Sprint 6CSPY option risk

Theta and vega: time decay and volatility exposure

Theta estimates how the option value changes as time passes. Vega estimates how the option value changes when implied volatility changes. Together, theta and vega help students understand the central tradeoff in many option positions: time can work against the holder, while volatility can either rescue or damage the position.

Analyst purpose: Students should explain whether the position is more exposed to time decay or volatility change, and whether the option needs a volatility thesis to be defensible.
