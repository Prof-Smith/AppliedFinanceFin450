Sprint 6FSPY option risk

Hedging lab: from Greek exposure to hedge plan

A hedge plan translates sensitivity analysis into an action: how many shares to buy or sell, when to rebalance, and what risks remain even after the hedge is placed. Delta hedging can reduce first-order directional exposure, but gamma, theta, vega, and execution costs remain important.

Analyst purpose: Students should understand that a hedge is a managed process, not a one-time fix. The hedge should be evaluated for residual risk, rebalancing needs, and communication clarity.
