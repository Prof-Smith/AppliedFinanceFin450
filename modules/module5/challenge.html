SPY Greeks Risk Management Note

You are preparing a concise risk note for an SPY option position. The investment committee does not only want to know what the option is worth. It wants to know what can move the position, whether a hedge is needed, when the hedge should be rebalanced, and which risks remain after hedging.

Required analytical spine: position summary → delta and gamma exposure → theta and vega tradeoff → rho materiality → scenario stress test → hedge plan → residual risk and responsible communication.

Assessment Rubric

Module 5 case scoring guide

Greek interpretation, 25%

Accurately explains delta, gamma, theta, vega, and rho in practical risk language.

Scenario analysis, 20%

Uses stress-test evidence to identify primary risk driver and adverse scenarios.

Hedge plan, 20%

Provides a clear hedge target, trade needed, rebalance trigger, and residual risk explanation.

Materiality judgment, 15%

Distinguishes major risk drivers from secondary exposures.

Responsible communication, 10%

Communicates uncertainty, limitations, and execution concerns clearly.

Professional memo quality, 10%

Writes concisely with evidence-backed recommendation and clear structure.
