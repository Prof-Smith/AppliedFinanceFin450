Inspect the sensitivity profile of an option

The Greeks turn an option price into a risk map. Delta estimates directional exposure, gamma shows how delta changes, theta captures time decay, vega measures volatility exposure, and rho measures rate sensitivity.

Analyst purpose: Students should be able to say what moves the option, which input matters most, and how the exposure should be communicated to a decision maker.
