Sprint 6BSPY option risk

Delta and gamma: directional exposure and curvature risk

Delta estimates the first-order price change of an option when SPY moves. Gamma measures how quickly delta changes as SPY moves. A position can look hedged at the current price but become increasingly exposed as the underlying moves, especially when gamma is high.

Analyst purpose: Students should translate delta and gamma into a hedge interpretation: how many shares are needed to neutralize current directional exposure, and why that hedge can break as SPY moves.
