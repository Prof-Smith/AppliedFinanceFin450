Rho and rates: when interest-rate sensitivity matters

Rho estimates how an option value changes when interest rates change. For short-dated equity options, rho is often smaller than delta, gamma, theta, or vega. However, rho becomes more meaningful when maturity is longer, rates are changing, or the option is deep in or out of the money.

Analyst purpose: Students should learn not to overemphasize rho when it is immaterial, but also recognize when rate exposure deserves disclosure in an option risk note.
