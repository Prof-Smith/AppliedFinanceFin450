Greeks, Hedging, and Option Risk Communication

Module 5 continues the option desk sequence by asking how an option position changes when the market moves. Module 4 answered what an option may be worth. Module 5 asks how the value changes with stock price, volatility, time, and rates, and how an analyst should communicate that risk responsibly.

Design principle: This module should not become a generic risk-management module. It should stay focused on option sensitivity, hedge interpretation, scenario communication, and a concise Greeks risk note.
