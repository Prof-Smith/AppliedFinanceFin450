Scenario risk: translate Greeks into stress-test language

Greeks are local sensitivity measures. Scenario analysis turns those measures into a more practical risk conversation by combining SPY price shocks, volatility shocks, time decay, and rate shocks into one stress-test view. This page helps students move from isolated Greeks to a position-level risk story.

Analyst purpose: Students should identify which scenario creates the largest gain or loss, which risk driver matters most, and what a decision maker should monitor.
